Call for a camera inspection when you see these signs.

Sewage smell inside or outside If you can smell sewage indoors or in the yard, don’t wait. A damaged line exposes your family to harmful gases.
Multiple slow drains One slow drain is a local clog. Several across the house points to the main sewer line.
Toilets gurgling Bubbling or gurgling means air is trapped in the line, from a blockage or a break in the pipe.
Unusually green patches in the yard A bright green stripe across the lawn often means sewage is leaking underground and fertilising the grass above it.
Sewage backing up into tubs or toilets Raw sewage coming back up is a full blockage. Stop using water and call us immediately.
Home is 30+ years old Older DFW homes often have cast iron or clay pipes past their lifespan. A camera inspection is worth it.
Buying a home in DFW A standard home inspection stops at the walls. A camera scope of the sewer line before you close tells you whether a five-figure repair is waiting for you.
Your insurer asks for documentation Insurance and warranty claims often require proof of the pipe’s condition. We record the footage and give you the report.
